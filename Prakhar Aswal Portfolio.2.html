Retailer and dealer sales performance analysis

UltraTech Cement · Summer internship · Power BI

Problem
Understand how a network of retailers and dealers performs: who sells the most, who sells the least, and how much dealers contribute.
Approach
Built the analysis in Power BI, with KPI cards, a ranking of retailers by sales and supporting tables, covering 172 retailers and 19 dealers.
Outcome
The top 5 retailers account for about 11.1% of total sales, led by Sardar Building Material Supplier. Sales are concentrated in a few retailers, which I flagged as a business risk if their sales decline.
